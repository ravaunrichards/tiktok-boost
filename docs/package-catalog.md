# Package catalogue

## Campaign objectives
1. Follower growth — Community Interaction / Follows where the advertiser account is eligible.
2. Video views — Video Views / focused-view optimization where available.
3. Reach and awareness — Reach and impression planning.
4. Profile visits — Community Interaction / page-visit optimization where available.
5. Website traffic — destination traffic planning.
6. Lead generation — lead campaigns with privacy and destination review.
7. Creator collaborations — manually reviewed creator partnership briefs.

## Package sizes
Each objective has Micro, Small, Medium, Large, and Enterprise. Displayed USD amounts are suggested starting media budgets, not final service fees, live auction quotes, or guaranteed results. The operator must confirm actual price, taxes, fees, advertiser eligibility, and budget before taking payment.

## Follower milestones
Planning milestones are 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 50,000, 100,000, 250,000, 500,000, and 1,000,000. They communicate the customer's ambition. TikBoost does not sell or guarantee those follower totals.

## Engagement analytics
Follows, profile visits, reach, impressions, focused views, video views, likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks, and leads are distinct metrics. Metrics can be selected for reporting; artificial interaction quantities must not be sold, automated, or guaranteed.
